Tournament Story & Legacy

🇶🇦 FIFA World Cup Qatar 2022™ Overview

Nov 20 – Dec 18, 2022 32 Nations · 64 Matches

A Historic Tournament in the Middle East

The 2022 FIFA World Cup in Qatar was the 22nd edition of the world's most prestigious football tournament. It made history as the first FIFA World Cup ever hosted in the Arab World and Middle East, and the first held in November–December to provide optimal playing temperatures.

32 national teams competed across 64 matches in 8 architectural masterpiece stadiums. Qatar 2022 set an all-time tournament record with 172 total goals scored (avg. 2.69 goals/match), delivering unprecedented excitement to over 3.4 million live spectators and billions of worldwide viewers.

Unforgettable Stories & Milestones

👑
Argentina's 3rd World Cup Crown Lionel Messi captained Argentina to glory in a legendary Final (3–3 AET, 4–2 pen vs France), earning his first World Cup trophy and 2nd Golden Ball.
🇲🇦
Morocco's Historic Semi-Final Run The Atlas Lions became the first African and Arab country to reach the World Cup semi-finals, knocking out Spain and Portugal.
👟
Kylian Mbappé's Golden Boot France's Mbappé scored 8 goals in the tournament, including a dramatic hat-trick in the Final, winning the Golden Boot.
⚡
Cutting-Edge Innovations Introduced Semi-Automated Offside Technology (SAOT) with connected match balls, solar pitch-cooling, and demountable modular stadiums (Stadium 974).
